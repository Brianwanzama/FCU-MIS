Other savings

Other savings is extra money you save with FCU, apart from your regular monthly deposits. FCU places it in the unit trust, and the interest it earns is credited to you every month. Money kept in a bank account earns nothing like this. Your regular deposits and the group's own unit trust earnings are not affected. The interest you earn can be withdrawn at any time.
